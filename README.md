# Vendor Risk Assessment – CloudDocs AI

## Overview

This project simulates a real-world third-party vendor cybersecurity risk assessment from the perspective of a GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance) Analyst.

The assessment evaluates CloudDocs AI, a fictional cloud-based document storage provider, to identify security and compliance risks associated with handling sensitive business data.

---

## Frameworks Referenced

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
- ISO/IEC 27001
- SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria

---

## Project Deliverables

- Project Overview
- Vendor Security Questionnaire
- Risk Register
- Risk Assessment Report
- Executive Summary
- Control Mapping
- Control Testing
- Access Control Policy

---

## Key Risks Identified

- Incomplete MFA enforcement
- Weak subcontractor oversight
- Lack of formal patch management timelines
- Limited suspicious login monitoring
- Absence of SOC 2 Type II certification
- Inadequate disaster recovery testing

---

## Outcome

The vendor received an overall risk rating of Medium to High and was recommended for conditional approval pending remediation of identified control gaps.
